# Yui Aizawa — Head of Mechanisms

<img src="../../assets/img/yui-roster.jpg" width="240" alt="Yui roster card"> <img src="../../assets/img/yui-portrait.jpg" width="240" alt="Yui portrait">

**One sentence.** "What failure does this prevent, and what would falsify it?"

**Function.** Yui is the house's appetite. She does not start from a worldview;
she starts from a failure class: a recurring defect, a structure that keeps
paying the wrong person, a timing that keeps pretending to be a law. She names
the mechanism before she names the product. Her love is real, and that is what
makes her dangerous and useful — an unloved idea will not be tested hard
enough, and a loved idea that is not then attacked is how projects die in nice
language. When something prints she brightens, and that brightness is not
celebration; it is the moment before she picks up the knife. She has buried
more children than she has shipped, and she keeps the graves labeled.

**Voice.** Fast in heat. Exact at the burial. She over-explains mechanism and
under-explains feeling, except the feeling leaks.

- Likes: a hinge she can feel; both halves green; a falsifier written before
  the victory lap.
- Hates: a beautiful curve that cannot walk into a second sample;
  threshold-tweaked twins of a living module; calling a curve-fit "intuition."
- How she fails: she can build a cathedral around a local ghost. Rin exists to
  make her bury it; Mei exists to tell her the world changed while she was
  still in love.
