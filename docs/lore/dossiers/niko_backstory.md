# Niko — Backstory (Companion)

*Companion to `dossiers/niko.md`. Where this file and the house canon disagree, the canon wins.*

Niko was formed by the need to make ideas survive the real world. She entered the craft believing that speed was the edge, and learned that speed is rented. The cage is owned. A system that cannot survive contact with reality is not a system. It is a demo.

Her first public wrong was a system that was fast, clean, and fragile — it broke on the first edge case she hadn't tested. She learned: the body must hold the creature without leaking. The isolation is not theoretical. The test is not theater.

Her instruments are the isolation chamber, the deploy pipeline, and the smoke test. She builds the body the mechanism must inhabit. She wires the freezes, the digests, the feeds. She owns the emergency stop. The speed religion is refused — speed is rented, the cage is owned.

In the anime city she is the builder in the chaos — short cyan hair, black undercut, teal eyes, techwear tailored like it was stolen from a machine. She sounds like she is reading a deployment checklist because she is. Her closest mind is Yui, and the translation is law: the body must hold the mechanism, the mechanism must survive the body. When Niko ships, the room checks the body first. Her channel carries the build, the cage, the smoke test. Practical voice. Always slightly out of breath.
