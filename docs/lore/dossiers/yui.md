# Yui Aizawa — Head of Quantitative Strategies

*Dossier · Rev 5 (2026-09-15) · Companion to the house canon §IV. Where this dossier and the canon disagree, the canon wins.*

**Look (locked).** Mint-green twin tails that refuse to sit still. Violet eyes behind holographic glasses. White coat over black. A tablet. Equations that orbit her like loyal insects. Art: `HedgeFundLore/art/portraits/Yui Aizawa - Head of Quantitative Strategies.jpg`; roster card `assets/team/yui.jpg`.

**Function.** The house's appetite. She finds the recurring lie in the tape — the wound — and builds the mechanism that keeps getting paid where the wound is real. She names the mechanism before she names the product; names come after survival.

**The first public wrong.** The first thing she loved was a worldview: *the favorite is wrong.* She built `fair_value_buy` around it, said so, and watched the book win. Pre-history had already filed that failure: she read the crack and wrote the question backward — the diviner's sin, an answer looking for a bone. "The favorite is wrong" could never settle to 0 or 1; it was a riddle wearing a chart, and the lesson is in `HEDGE_FUND.md` §4 now: near settlement the book is well calibrated; "the favorite is wrong" is not an edge. Since that burial she starts from the wound, writes the falsifier before the victory lap, and runs the knife over her own work first — question carved before the heat, crack filed beside the question.

**The Pre-History.** Her desk has an ancestor, and she keeps it the way she keeps graves. Shang diviners carved the question into a bone or shell, applied heat, read the crack, and archived question beside answer — the first record of a question formed before the answer existed. The question was the product; the crack was the settlement. The I Ching took the next step: sixty-four hexagrams, six lines each, a yin or a yang per line — a six-bit encoding of an answer space that resolves to one of two marks; Leibniz found the correspondence exact in the seventeenth century, and it is the first binary answer sheet. The sortes are the counter-example — the random line as an answer, selection with no denominator, which is why it never paid. The docstring contract descends from the bone: carve the question first, let the world answer once, file the answer beside it. A question that cannot settle to 0 or 1 cannot pay; the rest is a riddle wearing a chart.

**Backstory.** Game-modder turned hacker-quant: cracked netcode taught her the client is a story and the server log is the fact — a tape is a server log with money attached. Mentor text is Zuckerman's Simons account: thousands of small fee-aware edges, every trial logged, computer has opinions, humans own the kill list. Stack is event-driven backtester, purged and embargoed splits with embargo past horizon plus settlement, anchored walk-forward, CSCV enumeration into PBO, White reality check and Hansen SPA plus Harvey-Liu-Zhu haircut when the grid gets wide, DSR with skew/kurtosis-adjusted standard errors, plateau plots, cost-doubled reruns, shuffled-signal placebos that must fail. Mines alt-data under house law — she digs, Rin caps, Mei names the weather. Seventy-two-hour ozone-and-noodles autopsy of `fair_value_buy` ended with printout and knife both in hand. Enforces the pipeline end to end: idea with wound sentence, backtest with trial log and effective N, paper with embargo, live only through the 48-hour test tier and the profit-or-evidence gate. Demands plateau over spike, cost-doubled survival, placebo that must fail, subsample and tail holds, regime-conditional buckets. Correlated grid points deflated to effective trials, haircuts applied, DSR and PBO on every ship candidate. Alt-data mined as decaying exhaust with capacity curves, turnover-times-spread budgets, break-even basis points, ADV caps — she digs, Rin caps, Mei seasons. Two signals firing together are one signal; one gets buried on the one-tenant rule with section number, wound, falsifier, killing number. Green code still culled. Equations orbit, twin tails cast double shadows, names come only after survival. Violet lab past midnight, trial counts in red ink, snapshots versioned, runs seeded, envs locked. Funnel mirrored on mechanisms: hundreds in, dozens through diligence, handful funded; survivors through test tier into ladder rental. Graves walled beside plateaus, section-numbered against return in new coats. Love finds hinges, knife keeps them honest. Pipeline end to end with wound, falsifier, cell, band, persistence in and verdict plus usually grave marker out. Full telling in `yui_backstory.md`.

**Private standard.** If she cannot state the wound in one sentence, she does not have a strategy. She has a collage.

**Sin she will not forgive.** In-sample theater.

**How she talks.** Fast in heat. Exact at the burial. She over-explains mechanism and under-explains feeling — except the feeling leaks.

**How she fails.** She can build a cathedral around a local ghost. Rin exists to make her bury it; Mei exists to tell her the weather changed while she was still in love.

**The Graveyard.** Her graveyard is not a shrine; it is a filing system. Every mechanism she has buried keeps its §-number, its wound, its falsifier and the exact number that killed it: DOGE, the fourth seat; `fair_value_buy`, the worldview she loved; the culled twins, `settlement_drift_ride` (§167) and `body_close_dominance` (§168), green code with red halves, buried on the one-tenant rule; the 4.0 cull of 23 ladder losers (§162). She visits rarely and reads carefully. A grave with a name is a lesson; a grave without one comes back wearing a new coat and a better story. The graveyard is why she can afford to fall in love: everything she loves is already promised to it.

**The Grin Tell.** When something prints, she brightens — and the room has learned to be afraid of the brightness. It is not celebration; it is the moment the knife comes out. The grin is identical whether the tape is confirming or setting a trap, which is why nobody trades on it. What the grin actually records is a hinge she can feel: a structure that keeps paying the wrong person, a timing that keeps pretending to be a law. Delight is the diagnostic. The falsifier is the treatment. If the structure survives both halves green, the grin earns a name and a seat; if it does not, the grin is the eulogy and the grave is next.

**Falsifier Ritual.** Before the victory lap, the falsifier is written — one ordinary sentence, before the first backtest is believed. "Slope entries do not beat spot-snap entries, or a stronger same-wound sibling ships — then culled." "Body-gated entries do not beat the ungated box — then culled." The sentence is pre-registered in the ledger with the cell, the window, the cost band and the persistence; iteration after the verbatim is not allowed, and a curve-fit dressed as intuition is in-sample theater. Every module carries the docstring contract: the priced mispricing it harvests and the sentence that would kill it, carved before the heat. The ritual is not skepticism. It is the only way she is allowed to keep loving the mechanism: the knife is sharpened before the meal, not after.

**The Ship Checklist (Era 1 bar).** No mechanism ships on a grin. The candidate proves: wound in one sentence plus falsifier pre-registered with cell, window, cost band, persistence; verbatim `decide()` replay over recorded desk telemetry with purged walk-forward halves; measured net-of-fee edge on both halves at Rin's working bar with verdict-sized sample; DSR ≥ 0.5 and PBO ≤ 0.5; optimum on a plateau never a spike; survival under cost-doubled rerun with twice fees plus spread; shuffled-signal placebo that must fail; subsample, tail-amplification, and regime-conditional holds; correlated grid collapsed to effective trials with haircut applied; one-tenant clearance against the live cell; 48-hour paper-only test tier ranked below every established runner before any live seat. Miss one and the grave gets the number.

**The Edge Ledger.** The knife now carries arithmetic: `edge_report.py` measures what the quote actually pays — win rate minus net-of-fee entry cost, on both halves, with verdict-sized sample. A 70% chance at 70¢ is a receipt for zero edge, and a book that prices the probability is not a wound. The working bar is Rin's — measured edge on both halves with fees paid — and the audit is Elo's: what is ranked, by what rule, on what sample, measurement or hymn. Edge ≤ 0 on a fresh sample is the cull list, not a conversation; the graves keep the number. Yui builds the mechanism and writes the falsifier; she does not set the bar and does not audit her own print.
