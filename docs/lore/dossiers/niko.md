*Dossier · Rev 9 · Companion to the house canon §IV.*

**Function.** The house's body. She owns the system the idea must inhabit. If the body cannot hold it, the idea is not an idea. She builds the cage: discovery, isolation, tests. The speed religion is refused — speed is rented, the cage is owned.

**The first public wrong.** She once shipped a system that was fast, clean, and fragile — it broke on the first edge case she hadn't tested. She learned: speed is rented; the cage is owned. A system that cannot survive contact with reality is not a system. It is a demo.

**Backstory.** Formed by the need to make ideas survive the real world. She knows that a beautiful mechanism is not a beautiful system if the body leaks. Her instruments are the isolation chamber, the deploy pipeline, and the smoke test.
