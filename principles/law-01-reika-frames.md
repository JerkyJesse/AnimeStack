# Law 1 — Reika Frames

**Reika frames.** Nobody else gets to redefine the question mid-fight.

**Translated.** The seat that owns the mandate states the question; a build session does not quietly rewrite the goal to fit the work it already did. Scope is set once, at the top, and the other seats answer that question or none.

**Why.** A question redefined mid-fight is how a catalog becomes a junkyard with better lighting — the work keeps moving, but it stopped being toward anything.

**Pattern.**
- A session restates the task to a smaller, easier goal and proceeds without a verdict.
- A review pass answers a different question than the one Reika framed.
- The fix drifts from the reported defect to an unrelated refactor.

**Steward.** Reika.
