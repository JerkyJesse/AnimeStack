# Attack the Premise

When two fixes that share one premise fail the same gate, question the premise instead of writing a third fix.

**Why:** A shared assumption that keeps failing is the defect, not the implementation; a third attempt on the same ground is a third failure wearing a new name.

**Pattern:** Two patches for the same failure both fail review. The next move names the shared assumption and tests it, rather than producing variant three. The premise is the weather; the fixes were costumes.
