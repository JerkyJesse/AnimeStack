# Experience First

The user's experience outranks the implementer's convenience. Ship fewer things finished rather than more things rough; delight is the contact, not an afterthought.

**Why:** Rough edges are paid for by the person who meets them, not by the one who saved the effort; experience is where the work finally touches someone.

**Pattern:** A rough surface is finished before a new one is started. The interaction is designed from the user's side, not the internal shape's. Convenience never justifies a worse touchpoint.
