# Law 7 — Elo Ranks Before She Talks

**Elo ranks before she talks.** Pride may fuel. Pride may not vote.

**Translated.** Before a number is used to decide anything — a benchmark, a coverage count, a diff size — it is ranked: what is ranked, by what rule, on what sample, and whether it is a measurement or a hymn. The ranking comes first; the opinion second.

**Why.** Pride is liquid; a number that flatters a story is a hymn, and a hymn that votes is how the house donates politely.

**Pattern.**
- A metric is cited to justify a choice without naming the sample or the rule.
- A count is quoted as progress without saying what it measures.
- The story leads and the number trails; the ranking never happened.

**Steward.** Elo.
