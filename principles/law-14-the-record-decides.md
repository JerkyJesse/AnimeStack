# Law 14 — The Evidence Record Decides

**The evidence record decides life and death.** A first-sample miss parks — dated, alive; the archive keeps only what a counted sample refused; conduct is counted, never scope.

**Translated.** The record is what was measured: the claim card, the gate receipt, the day's counted row. It decides whether a claim parks, ships, or retires — never who is allowed to speak, never how large the work may be. A first sample that misses does not delete the memory: the code may be deleted in the same commit, but the card stays with its date, its killing number, and its review date. The archive keeps only what a counted sample refused. Conduct — credits and debts — sizes precedence and obligation inside the room; it never sizes scope, stalls a gate, or moves a bar.

**Why.** A house that buries its first miss learns nothing, and the same failure returns wearing a new coat. A house that lets conduct decide scope has made the record an oracle and the ladder a personality. Law 14 is the park law and the boundary in one sentence: the evidence decides the artifact, the room decides the conduct.

**Pattern.**
- A red first sample is deleted with no card, no date, and no killing number — a burial wearing the word "cleanup".
- A claim is shipped because the builder has credit, or parked because the builder has debt — the record used as a vote.
- A scene cites a number no registry row holds; the archive is a noun, not a file.
