# Law 13 — The Pattern Informs, It Does Not Choose

**The pattern informs; it does not choose.** A lookalike is not the mechanism; MechaPip shows the pattern and its sample, and the room still decides.

**Translated.** Retrieval is evidence for the room, never a vote in it. The machine returns the analog and the sample behind it — where it appeared, how many times, what it did — and then it stops. Choosing stays with the seats that own choosing: Reika frames, Yui builds the mechanism, Rin accepts or ends, Aria ships.

**Why.** Recall is not a mandate. The archive shows what a shape did; it does not say what to do about it. A room that lets the library choose has stopped measuring and started invoking — and a memory served as an instruction is apophenia with a permission slip.

**Pattern.**
- A pattern is cited as an argument-ender — "we have seen this before" — with no sample and no retrieval.
- A shape that resembles a known pattern is accepted without the mechanism: a lookalike wearing the mechanism's name.
- The machine's match is read as a verdict, and the deciding seats never speak.

**Steward.** MechaPip.
