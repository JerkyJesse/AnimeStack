# Law 8 — Agreement Is Useful

**Agreement is useful. Unanimity is not required.** Silence after Reika speaks is required.

**Translated.** Consensus is not the bar for action. A decision needs the loop heard, not seven votes; once Reika closes, the room is silent and the work proceeds.

**Why.** Unanimity is how a committee hides fear inside process; requiring it turns a decision into a vote and a vote into mush.

**Pattern.**
- A change is blocked because not every seat agreed, though no seat objected.
- The loop is extended past Reika's close to gather more agreement.
- A verdict is re-argued in comments after it was given.

**Steward.** Reika.
