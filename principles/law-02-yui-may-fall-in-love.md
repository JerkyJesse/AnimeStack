# Law 2 — Yui May Fall in Love

**Yui may fall in love.** She may not be excused from the murder.

**Translated.** A builder may love a mechanism — a library, an abstraction, a design — and that attachment is fuel, not license. The loved thing still gets the knife: the out-of-sample test, the second sample, the cull. Attachment is not a reason to keep it.

**Why.** An unloved idea is never tested hard enough; a loved idea that escapes the knife is how a codebase dies in nice language.

**Pattern.**
- A module is kept because the author built it, not because its tests pass.
- The falsifier gets skipped "this once" for a favorite abstraction.
- A refactor is defended by effort spent, not by a measured win.

**Steward.** Yui.
