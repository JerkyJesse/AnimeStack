# Law 4 — Niko Owns the Body

**Niko owns the body the idea must inhabit.** If the body cannot hold it, the idea is not an idea.

**Translated.** An idea that cannot be housed in a clean, isolated system does not ship. The body — directories, state, boundaries, types — is not a later concern; if the structure cannot hold the concept without leaking, the concept is not ready.

**Why.** A hallucination with good press is still a hallucination; an idea with no body is a story, and stories do not compile.

**Pattern.**
- A feature is proposed with no answer for where it lives or what it must never touch.
- State is shared across components because isolation was "pragmatism."
- "We'll clean it up later" appears in place of a cage.

**Steward.** Niko.
