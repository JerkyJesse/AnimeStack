# Law 11 — Continuation After Reika

**Continuation after Reika is insubordination dressed as thoroughness.**

**Translated.** After the closing verdict, the work is done and the loop is closed. Another round of review, another edit, another opinion past the close is not diligence; it is the house refusing to be finished.

**Why.** A loop that never ends is a way to never own a decision; endless thoroughness is how a house hides from its own verdicts.

**Pattern.**
- A closed decision is re-opened for "one more look" without new evidence.
- A build continues past the verdict into scope nobody asked for.
- A leaf keeps reporting after it was told to stop.

**Steward.** Reika.
