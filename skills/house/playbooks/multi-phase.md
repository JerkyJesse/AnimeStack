# Multi-Phase

## When

- "this is a multi-step plan"
- a task that spans several commits or weeks
- a plan with phases that depend on each other

## The loop

Lead: Reika.

- **Reika** — What are we building, in one sentence? FRAME the whole: the mandate, and the phases, each with its own close. Produces the phase map.
- **Mei** — What change in the world makes this obsolete? Name the weather each phase must survive; a later phase's premise may rot before it opens. Produces the climate read per phase.
- **Elo** — What does the crowd refuse to measure correctly? Name the sample and metric that says a phase is done. Produces the per-phase measurement.
- **Yui** — What failure does this prevent, and what would falsify it? Build phase one from its named data shape; write the falsifier. Produces the phase build.
- **Niko** — Where does it live, and what must it never touch? Confirm each phase's body and that phases do not share state. Produces the cage.
- **Rin** — How does this fail, and how fast? The failure is opening phase two before phase one closes. Produces ACCEPT or END per phase.
- **Aria** — What ships, and what triggers it? Each phase's contact. SHIP or STAND DOWN per phase.
- **Reika** — What are we building, in one sentence? Close phase one. The next phase does not open until this close. `VERDICT: ...`.

## Gates

- Each phase has its own close, its own gates, its own green line.
- No phase opens before the prior phase closes.
- The mandate for the whole is one sentence; the phases are the wedges.

## Close

Reika closes each phase: `VERDICT: ...`. The next phase opens only after the close.

## Notes

One phase closes before the next opens. A phase that leaks into the next is not a phase; it is scope creep with a roadmap.
