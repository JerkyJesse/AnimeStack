The playbooks · twelve doors

Playbooks

“What are we building, in one sentence?”

Twelve playbooks: the request matches one, the loop opens, and the room closes with a verdict.

The router reads the request and picks one playbook. The loop descends eight seats in order, one sentence each, and closes with a verdict. A build under a playbook carries its claim card.

Autonomous Run

"do this end to end without me" · a multi-step task with reversible and irreversible steps · the human is away and the work can proceed on its own judgment
