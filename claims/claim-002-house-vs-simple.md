## The failure

The pack's oldest claim is that the room is worth its cost. It is also the claim nobody has paid for. The falsifier has been pre-registered since the counts audit — a fresh sample, a control, both halves, a receipt — and no counted sample has ever been run against it. A claim that costs eight seats and has never been counted is the unseated memory again, this time wearing the pack's own badge.

This is the house's first public act under its own standard: it parks its own claim, dated, in the open.

## Why it parks and does not ship

The standard asks for a sample the claim never saw, a control that removes the mechanism, and both halves scored. None of the three exists for this claim. The pack can show its counts, its gates, its board, and its record; it cannot show a measured difference between the room and a plain session, because nobody has run the sample.

A first look that would ship on the strength of the pack's own design is in-sample theater with better typography. So the claim parks.

## The sample it must count

Ten fresh tasks, run after this page is read, by one hand in one week, half under the room and half plain. The unit is a task; the floor is ten; the halves are the first five and the last five in time order. A defect is counted when it escapes to the release candidate in one mode and is caught before contact in the other.

The window closes on the review date. If the sample is not collected by then, the card is re-dated, not quietly dropped — a park that misses its own date is the filing cabinet the standard refuses.

## The control

The same ten tasks, the same hand, the same week, the plain mode. The control is the change with the mechanism removed: if the plain session catches the same defects, the room was not the mechanism.

## What would retire it

A counted sample at the stated floor with no measured difference in either half. Then the room is prose wearing a coat, the card becomes an archive row with its killing number, and the pack says so in its own record. Until then the card is alive, dated, and owed an answer.
