```yaml
id: claim-001
claim: The claim standard turns a ship into a claim a second hand can audit — ten fields, one card, and a gate that refuses the sentence written after the result.

mechanism: docs/claim-standard.md and scripts/check-claim; law 10 and law 14; the card replaces the announcement.
sample: eleven gate cases — nine refusals, one clean card set, and the count and list modes — run by `python -m pytest tests/test_claim_standard.py -q` on 2026-11-05; floor eleven cases, stated before the suite was written.
control: the v1.17.0 tree with the standard absent: no claims/, no gate, no park law — a ship announced, never claimed.

disposition: SHIP pattern=P-001
```

## The failure

The v1.17.0 pack had the falsifier in its mouth and nowhere in its hands. Forty-eight seat files told a builder to write the falsifier before the victory lap; the tree held no claim card, no registry, no gate that could tell a claim from a hymn. A red first sample was "retired" — deleted, unrecorded, unnamed — and the next session rebuilt it from taste.

That is the unseated memory, `P-001`, wearing a public coat.

## The mechanism

One page (`docs/claim-standard.md`), one directory (`claims/`), one gate (`scripts/check-claim`). The card carries ten fields; the gate refuses the orderings that turn a test into a caption: a ship with a red half, a park with no date, a retire with no counted refusal, a control that is the mechanism, a THIN card quoting a result, a receipt whose path the tree cannot resolve.

Law 10 keeps the deletion: a losing mechanism leaves the tree in the same commit. Law 14 keeps the memory: the card stays, dated, with its red half and its review date. Deletion is not burial.

## The sample

The gate's own red-once suite. Nine refusals were watched red before the gate was trusted: missing falsifier, carved after the sample, a red half marked SHIP, a park with no review date, a retire with no counted refusal, a control identical to the mechanism, a THIN card quoting a result, a dead receipt path, a hymn token. Then the clean run on the live cards, the count, and the list.

The floor was eleven cases before the first test was written. It reproduces:

```
python -m pytest tests/test_claim_standard.py -q
```

## The control

The same tree one release earlier: `claims/` absent, the gate absent, the park law absent. A ship was an announcement and a red sample was a deletion. If the v1.17.0 tree had caught the nine refusals without a card, the card was not the mechanism. It could not: it had nowhere to put them.

## The halves

The formed-on half is the red: every refusal was written as a failing test first, and the gate was watched fail before it was believed. The fresh half is this tree: three cards, one per disposition, and a clean gate line. A green that arrives with its red in the same card is a measurement; a green that arrives alone is a hymn.
